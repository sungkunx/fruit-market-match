📖 How to Play

🎯 How to Play
Swipe a fruit up, down, left, or right to swap it with its neighbor. Line up 3 or more of the same fruit to pop them!
⚡ Score Tips
Bigger matches score more, and falling fruits can set off chain reactions for bonus points. Match the same fruit back-to-back to boost your multiplier faster!
⏱️ Time Bonus
Popping fruit adds time to the clock, and bigger groups and chain reactions add even more. The clock is capped at 30 seconds, so it never overflows.
